# Premium AI Prompt – Indian Payday Loan Mobile App UI/UX Design

Design a **modern, premium, high-converting Indian Payday Loan Mobile Application** from scratch with exceptional UI/UX. The app should look like a professionally designed fintech product—not an AI-generated template. Focus on trust, simplicity, speed, and a premium banking experience while maintaining a clean, minimal interface.

## Brand Identity

* Use the attached company logo throughout the app.
* The app color palette must be derived from the logo colors.
* Maintain consistent branding across all screens.
* Create a premium fintech identity similar to leading Indian finance apps.
* Design should feel trustworthy, secure, and modern.
